An endless runner you play with your whole body

CardioSurf turns your iPhone's front camera into a motion controller. Stand in front of it, start a level, and the game reads your real movements — leaning, jumping, ducking, stepping side to side — to steer your character down the track. It's a cardio workout that never feels like one.

🏃

Move to play

Jump to leap gaps, duck under obstacles, and step left or right to dodge. Every input is a real movement — the game only advances when you do.

🔒

Private by design

Pose tracking runs entirely on-device using Apple's Vision framework. Camera video is never recorded, saved, or uploaded — not to us, not to anyone.

🎮

Levels & modes

Progress through themed campaigns and game modes that ramp up the intensity as you get fitter, so there's always a new challenge waiting.

📈

Track your progress

Your runs, streaks, and scores sync to your account so you can pick up where you left off and watch your fitness climb over time.

🔥

Real sweat, real fun

A full-body cardio session disguised as a game. Short sessions, high energy — the kind of workout you actually look forward to.

🦊

Your hype-fox

Your fox mascot runs right alongside you, cheering every jump and celebrating every finish line. You're never grinding alone.

How it works

Up and running in under a minute.

1

Prop up your phone

Stand back a few steps so the front camera can see your whole body, with clear space around you to move safely.

2

Pick a level

Choose a campaign or game mode. The app calibrates to you on-device — no sign-up dance, no wearables needed.

3

Move to win

Jump, duck, and dodge to keep your runner alive. Chase high scores, build streaks, and break a real sweat.
